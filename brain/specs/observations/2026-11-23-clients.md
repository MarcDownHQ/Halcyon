# Live probe: which Innertube clients play a whole video, logged out, without a PO token

- **Date:** 2026-11-23, 09:51 to 10:52 UTC.
- **Network:** one residential IPv6 address (France, per `visitorData`), no proxy, no cookies, no login, no PO token anywhere.
- **Tooling:** plain `curl` 8.23 (OpenSSL TLS, no browser impersonation), one request at a time with 0.3 to 1.5 s gaps.
- **Budget:** 133 requests in total, each logged at the time with its UTC time, status, size and duration.
- **Raw data:** not kept. The request bodies, `/player` responses and headers, googlevideo response headers, playlists and captions were saved in a temporary scratch directory during the probe and have since been discarded; the tables below are what remains.
- **Clean room:** only the spec files (now in [`brain/specs/`](..)) were read. No third-party source code was opened.

## The answer in one paragraph

**Yes, for ordinary public VODs.** Logged out, with no PO token and no JavaScript, two clients play a whole video end to end through plain googlevideo URLs: **`visionos`** (up to 2160p60, VP9/AV1/H.264, Opus and AAC, DRC and multi-language audio) and **`ios_producer`** (H.264 up to 1080p plus AAC only).

- Their `adaptiveFormats` carry a ready `url`, already signed (`sig=` present), with **no `n`**.
- Every 10 MiB chunk of a 114 MiB 1080p stream and its audio came back 200/206, and so did the whole file read in one request.
- Both clients also return an `hlsManifestUrl` whose master, media playlists and segments all play without a token.
- **Requests over about 11 MiB are not refused but paced** to exactly 2× real time. So a single open-ended mpv read works but is slow to fill, while 10 MiB chunks arrive at 15 to 25 MiB/s.

Every other candidate either needs the `n`/signature JavaScript (`tv_samsung`, `mweb`, `web_safari`) or does not return streams at all (`tv_downgraded`, `web_embedded`).

**Two caveats.**
- **Live streams were refused to every client** ("Sign in to confirm you’re not a bot").
- **Twice, shortly after live `/player` calls, the whole IP got the same bot check on VODs too.** The first time it lasted 30 minutes or less. Fresh `visitorData` did not clear it, but already-issued media URLs kept working.

### `&range=` query versus `Range:` header (09:54:02 to 09:54:03)

| Test | Response | Speed |
|---|---|---|
| visionos 248, `Range: bytes=20971520-31457279` | `206 Partial Content`, `Content-Range: bytes 20971520-31457279/80329656` | 18.1 MiB/s |
| ios_producer 136, `&range=10485760-20971519` | `302` then `200 OK`, `Content-Length: 10485760`, **no `Content-Range`** | 15.1 MiB/s |

Both forms are honoured and equally fast. The query form answers `200` with just the slice. The header form answers a proper `206` with `Content-Range` and `Accept-Ranges: bytes`, which is what mpv/ffmpeg expect. Responses carry `Cache-Control: private, max-age=≈21270` and `Server: gvs 1.0`. A 302 to a sibling edge host happens on either form, so the client must follow redirects.

**Finding:** a response larger than somewhere between 11 MiB and 16 MiB is delivered at a pace of exactly **twice real time** for that format.
- Every whole file took 335.4 s, which is half of the 669.8 s duration, whatever its size.
- Partial reads take the same fraction of 335 s as their fraction of the file: 40 MiB of 57.8 MB is 72.5 %, and took 245.9 s; 27.8 of 57.8 MB is 48.1 %, and took 161.4 s.
- There is no fast initial burst: the 16 MiB read was paced from the first byte.
- Nothing was refused: no 403 and no truncation, on query or header ranges alike.

So mpv can play these URLs directly with an open-ended `Range` (2× real time is enough to play, and seeking works). The buffer just fills slowly. To fetch fast, as a relay or prefetcher would, issue ≤10 MiB ranges.

## Verdicts

| Client | Verdict (logged out, no PO token, no JS) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **`visionos`** | **Playable end to end** (VOD) | Pre-signed URLs without `n`. 2160p60 VP9/AV1, 1440p, 1080p60. Opus + AAC, DRC, multi-language audio (with `audioTrack` labels and `audioIsDefault`). The complete 114 MiB + 9 MiB download had every chunk 200/206, repeated on a second video. Its HLS (up to 2160p60, separate audio, a VTT subtitle rendition) plays. Captions work. Ranges > ~11 MiB are paced to 2× real time. Live refused (bot check). |
| **`ios_producer`** | **Playable end to end** (VOD), lower ceiling | Pre-signed URLs without `n`, but H.264 ≤1080p and AAC 139/140 only, original audio track only, no DRC, no captions block. The complete download was all 206. Its HLS (H.264 ≤1080p) plays. Live refused. |
| `tv_samsung` | **No**, without JS | Full itag set (to 2160p, DRC, voice boost, multi-audio), but every URL carries `n` (403 when unsolved), and the music video is `signatureCipher`. It would be playable only with an `n` and signature solver running the player JS. No HLS, no SABR URL. |
| `tv_downgraded` (both shapes) | **No** | `UNPLAYABLE` "The page needs to be reloaded." (yt-dlp shape). The SmartTube shape got `LOGIN_REQUIRED`, possibly because of the bot check. |
| `web_embedded` | **No** | `ERROR` "This video is unavailable" on every video, even with the embed page's `encryptedHostFlags`, client version and `clientScreen:"EMBED"`. |
| `mweb` | **No**, apart from captions | Adaptive formats are SABR-only. Only progressive 18 has a URL, and it has `n` (403) or a cipher. |
| `web_safari` | **No** (partly, with JS, for some videos) | Adaptive formats are SABR-only. Muxed HLS (≤1080p) appeared only on the music video, and its segments carry `/n/` and returned 403. |

**Design consequence:**
- **VOD:** a JS-free plugin can call `visionos` first and `ios_producer` second, carrying `visitorData` from one anonymous call. It can hand mpv either the direct adaptive URLs (EDL video + audio, with `Range` reads paced at 2× real time) or the HLS master (no rewriting needed for these two clients).
- **Live:** live is the open problem. Every client tried was bot-checked anonymously for live on 2026-11-23, and live calls coincided with a temporary IP-wide `/player` block of up to about 30 minutes. A plugin should treat `LOGIN_REQUIRED` "not a bot" as a back-off signal rather than rotating through clients.
